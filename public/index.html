Questions from head teachers

Anything not answered here, ask us during the demo.

Email us a question

Does Ishurio work on a phone?

Yes. Ishurio runs in the browser, so teachers mark the register and parents check marks on any phone with internet.

Can parents pay fees through Ishurio?

Not yet. The bursar records each mobile money, cash or bank payment, and Ishurio prints the receipt and updates the balance.

How does Ishurio send SMS notices?

Through your school's Africa's Talking account. Without one, notices still reach parents in their portal.

Does each school get its own Ishurio?

Yes. Every school runs on its own database, so no other school sees your pupils, marks or fees.

Which school staff can change marks and fees?

Teachers take attendance and enter marks. The bursar handles invoices and payments. Only the administrator manages users, classes and terms.

Which grades do Ishurio report cards use?

A from 80, B from 70, C from 60, D from 50, E from 40 and F below. Position is ranked by the average across subjects.

Can school teachers see fee balances?

No. The fee pages open only for the bursar and the administrator.

What happens when a school term ends?

The administrator adds the next term and marks it current. Marks and fees from earlier terms stay on each pupil's record.
